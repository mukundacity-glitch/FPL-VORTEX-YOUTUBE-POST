# FPL VORTEX — YouTube editorial voice (project-local)

## Audience
Fantasy Premier League managers seeking accurate gameweek analysis, understandable projections, injury context, fixtures, player value and actionable decisions.

## Voice and delivery
British English; confident, concise, energetic and evidence-led rather than sensational.
Existing narration uses Microsoft Edge TTS en-GB-RyanNeural.
Use full club names in speech (for example, "Brighton and Hove Albion").
One point per beat and no repeated claims or duplicate narration.

## Format
Preserve the existing five-scene Day 1 structure: Gameweek Brief; Gameweek Review; Players & Fixtures; Numbers & Market Outlook; Value Board.
Readable 4K player portraits, club crests and legible statistics; respect the original FPL VORTEX branding.

## Claims and data
Use official FPL endpoints and verified gameweek inputs for price, injury and availability, transfers, fixtures and player selections.
Label projected points and five-gameweek forecasts as estimates.
Never invent results, prices, statistics, player availability, imagery, or sources.

## Publishing limits
These editorial skills produce drafts, QA suggestions and metadata. They never authorize publication.
Preserve existing Actions schedules, secrets, destinations, render behavior and YouTube Private-upload safeguards.
Never change a video's visibility from Private to Public without explicit permission.
